## The Problem

Astronomers collect more images than they can classify alone. They need help finding galaxy shapes, patterns, and unusual objects.

## The Challenge

Contribute to a real citizen-science project, then build a simple app that helps users classify galaxies.

1. Explore the main galaxy types: spiral, elliptical, and irregular.
2. Classify a selection of real images on Zooniverse.
3. Record the visual clues that helped your group decide.
4. Build a Scratch or Python app that asks questions and suggests a galaxy type.
5. Test the app with new images and improve unclear questions.

## Technologies

- Zooniverse;
- Scratch or Python;
- image classification.

## Final Goal

Present a working galaxy classification app and explain how your classifications contributed to astronomical research.
