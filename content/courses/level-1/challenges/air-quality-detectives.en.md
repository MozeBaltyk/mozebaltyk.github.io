## The Problem

Air pollution affects our health, but air quality is not measured everywhere.

## The Challenge

Build an air quality monitoring station, collect measurements around the school, and investigate how pollution changes throughout the day.

1. Connect a particulate matter sensor to an ESP32.
2. Read and save measurements at regular intervals.
3. Compare results from different times or suitable locations.
4. Create a chart or dashboard showing the trends.
5. Publish suitable measurements to an open citizen-science network.

## Technologies

- ESP32;
- particulate matter sensor;
- Python or Scratch;
- data visualisation.

## Final Goal

Build a working air quality station and present a dashboard that explains the pollution trends you observed.

## Resource

[Sensor.Community](https://sensor.community/en/)

> Do not publish personal information or precise private locations with the measurements. The station is an educational tool, not a medical device.
