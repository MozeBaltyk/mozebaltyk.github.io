A short guide for parents: what each stage teaches, how long it takes, and
how to help without doing it for them.

Here begins the **Courses** path — a friendly, project-based introduction to
computers and IT for children aged 10–13.

Go at your child's own pace: one short lesson at a time, always ending with
something they made themselves. Use the map below to see where they stand and
what comes next.
